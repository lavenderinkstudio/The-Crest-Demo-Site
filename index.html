A clifftop retreat

Arrive in the late afternoon. Leave your bags by the door, walk to the glass, and watch the swell roll in from Antarctica.

The Crest sits alone on a headland of limestone and saltbush, with nothing between the house and the horizon. Days here follow the weather: the cliff path in the morning, a long lunch in the sun, the fire lit as the light goes. At night you can hear the ocean from every room.

It is a place to slow down properly, for a weekend or for a week.

Inside the house

Every room turns toward the sea.

The living room

Floor-to-ceiling glass looks straight out to sea, so the weather becomes part of the room. There is a wood fire, a deep linen sofa, and a shelf of books for the rainy days.

The bath

A freestanding stone bath faces a full-height window. Run it at dusk, open something good from the local cellar door, and watch the light leave the water.

The bedrooms

Three bedrooms with king beds, linen sheets and wool blankets. Blackout blinds for long sleeps, and the sound of the sea to fall asleep to.

The deck

A wide timber deck with a fire pit and low chairs, made for long evenings under southern skies. On clear nights the Milky Way stretches right across the water.
